Why sleek and simple

The first prototype didn't look like anything, just a box with a spring. It worked, but nobody wanted to be seen with it clipped to their bag.

Golf gear tends to go one of two ways: loud logos or forgettable plastic. We wanted something that looked like it belonged next to the rest of your equipment: minimal, matte, and built with the same attention you'd expect from the clubs in your bag.
